Chengzhi Liu

I build multimodal agents that perceive, reason, remember, and act reliably in the real world.

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of California, Santa Barbara, advised by Prof. Xin Eric Wang. Before that I studied Information and Computing Science at the University of Liverpool, and in summer 2026 I was a Student Researcher at Google Cloud AI.

My work spans multimodal reasoning and hallucination, agent memory and self-improvement, proactive video agents, and the safety of large models and agentic systems.

Research

Agentic AI

Agents that plan, keep memory across long interactions, act in the world, and improve themselves from feedback.

Multimodal reasoning

Visual grounding, reasoning in latent space, streaming video understanding, and keeping models faithful to what they see.

Trustworthy AI

Safety evaluation for multimodal models and agent harnesses, hallucination mitigation, and alignment of reasoning models.
