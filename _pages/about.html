Chengzhi Liu.

Ph.D. Student / Multimodal Intelligence & Agents

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Santa Barbara, advised by Prof. Xin Eric Wang. I previously studied Information and Computing Science at the University of Liverpool and was a Student Researcher at Google Cloud AI in summer 2026.

My research explores how multimodal agents reason, remember, and act reliably. I work on multimodal reasoning and visual grounding, agent memory and self-improvement, and the safety of large models and agentic systems.
